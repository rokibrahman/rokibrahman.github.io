Mr. Raven

A private, self-hosted AI executive assistant for one person.

Mr. Raven is a personal assistant that its owner runs on his own server and uses through a private Telegram chat. It is not a commercial product, has no other users, and is not offered to the public.

What Mr. Raven does

  • Reads the owner's Gmail inbox and sorts messages into "reply today", "later" and "FYI".
  • Writes draft replies in Gmail Drafts for the owner to review and send himself.
  • Pulls tasks and deadlines out of emails and tracks them.
  • Sends the owner a short morning and evening brief.

What it never does

  • It never sends, deletes or forwards email on its own. Every change needs the owner's approval.
  • It never shares data with anyone other than the owner.
Google user data: Mr. Raven accesses only the Gmail data of its owner's own account, with his consent. See the Privacy Policy for details.
